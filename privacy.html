Privacy Policy

Velora Studio · Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. What Velora Studio is

Velora Studio is an invoicing and bookkeeping web app. You can use it without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses your account to keep your business data synced across your devices.

2. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and basic profile information (name and profile picture) as provided by Google. We use these only to identify your account.

The business data you enter (customers, invoices, payments, expenses and settings) is stored by you on your device, and, if you sign in, a copy is stored in our cloud database linked to your account.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to sign you in, to sync your data between your devices, and to keep your data safe. We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not share your data with third parties for marketing.

4. Where data is stored

Cloud data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database service, and is protected so that each user can only access their own data.

5. Your choices

You can sign out at any time from Settings. Signing out keeps your data on your device. You can also export a backup file from Settings. To delete your cloud data, contact us using the email below and we will remove it.

6. Google user data

Velora Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
